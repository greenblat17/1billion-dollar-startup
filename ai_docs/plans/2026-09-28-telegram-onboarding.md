# Telegram voice onboarding

Status: implemented on `feature/telegram-onboarding`; automated verification below. Live DEV acceptance is still required before production deployment.

## Agreed experience

Only new Telegram users automatically enter onboarding. Resolve eligibility before recording their first new funnel event: an existing funnel/chat record means the old greeting and ordinary conversation continue. `/onboarding` explicitly starts a fresh attempt for anyone.

1. `/start` sends the short English invitation and inline `Let’s chat 👋` button. Name comes from Telegram, with a nameless fallback. No greeting audio until the button is pressed.
2. The button sends a spoken introduction: Speaky says this is her voice and asks them to tell her about themselves. The voice caption is `🎙 Reply with a voice message in English` and `You can talk about your work, studies, hobbies — anything you like.` The same voice carries `0:00 / 2:00` and `Subtitles` on one row. After each later answer the clock is removed from the previous voice and attached to the latest one. It counts recognized speech. Once it reaches 2:00 and the introduction continues, the button reads `2:00+`. Tapping the clock sends `🎙 This tracks how much English you've spoken. Around 2 minutes is usually enough for me to get to know you a little.` Speaky does not mention it.
3. A voice answer gets an English voice reply. If there is a real mistake, one card comes first: a soft line for the type, the phrase as it was said, and the phrase to say instead. Grammar says `Правильно:`, a wrong word says `Лучше здесь сказать:`, and unidiomatic phrasing says `Естественнее:`. Speaky and the buttons are English; these correction labels stay Russian. No mistake means no card. The first voice of the attempt also gets a ❤ reaction; later voices do not, including after a silent first recording. The user voice that closes the attempt gets 🔥. The reply is a full turn: a specific warm reaction, then one question. The code still picks the next missing fact — work, then free time, then why English, then a personal follow-up — but the turn is not allowed to be only that question. No biography, no shared-interest claim, no Russian hint under the question, no timer.
4. The closing voice is not another question. The frame is fixed: `You know what, I really enjoyed talking with you. [Optional personal callback.] I feel like I know you a little better now.` then `And I’ve got a pretty good sense of your English too. Let me show you what I noticed.` The callback is one sentence only when a transcript has a specific detail, such as the person's own startup. Ordinary answers (`I work as a developer`, `I like movies`, `I need English for work`) get no callback. The smile stays in the subtitle; it is not sent to speech synthesis. The closing voice does not carry the clock. Its row is `See my results` and `Subtitles`. The level stays hidden until they tap `See my results`, which sends it once. The last user voice in the attempt gets a 🔥 reaction; the first voice still gets ❤ unless that same recording is the one that closes, in which case 🔥 replaces it. A correction card for the last answer still comes first when there is one. The level button is `See what I noticed →`, which edits the message into Grammar. If a CEFR letter exists it shows the letter and band name (`A1 Beginner`, `A2 Elementary`, `B1 Intermediate`, `B2 Upper-Intermediate`, `C1 Advanced`, `C2 Proficient`) plus an evidence-based explanation of the estimate. If the letter is null the screen says `I don't have a clear level from this chat yet.` and an explanation of why the speech sample is insufficient. `See what I noticed →` edits that message into Grammar, then Vocabulary, then Fluency. Each skill slide shows one sentence about this chat and a score the code maps from the model's band and low/mid/high position: A1 12/20/27, A2 32/38/43, B1 47/52/57, B2 63/70/77, C1 83/90/94. The model does not choose a raw 0–100. A skill with no band shows `Not enough from this chat yet.` instead of a number. Overall CEFR stays a separate judgment and is not an average of the three scores. The same table maps that letter plus low/mid/high to an overall score. The level screen shows the score, a 20-cell bar of the full scale, and how many points remain until the next band's low, such as `6 points to B2`. C2 and a missing letter have no score and no points line. `Continue →` then shows `B1 · 57/100 → B2` when that next band exists. Slides use a blank line between blocks, a bold score, and a heading emoji. Confidence is stored on the attempt and is not shown. Grammar and vocabulary show up to five `wrong → better` lines, repeats first, never invented and never simply the first five in time. Grammar is `kind=grammar`. Vocabulary is `kind=word`, then `kind=natural`. Fluency is not pairs: pace, long pauses of at least 1.0s inside one recording, filler tokens `um uh uhm umm er erm ah ahh hmm mm mhm`, and the longest stretch without such a pause. Rows that need word timings are omitted when those timings are missing; pace then uses the transcript. `Continue →` sends a new message asking for `☕ 5 min/day`, `✨ 10 min/day`, or `🔥 15 min/day`, each on its own row. The choice is stored at `practice-goal:{sessionId}` with no TTL, outside the attempt, so `/onboarding` does not erase it. The reply is a new message: `{n} minutes a day. Deal 🤝`, then `Keep talking 🎙` (ordinary continuation voice) and `See you tomorrow` (`See you tomorrow. I'll be here when you're ready.`). The level is still never spoken. Reminders do not yet use the saved minutes.
5. The next voice is ordinary conversation. Old `Продолжить разговор →` buttons still work; new attempts do not send that button.

## Timing and boundaries

- Count whole recordings with recognized speech, including pauses, using STT duration with Telegram duration as fallback. This is not measured speaking time.
- Before the time threshold, ask about missing profile fields: work, free time, and why English. "I don't work" or "no hobbies" fills the field; filling all fields is not required to finish.
- Close when recognized speech has reached 120 seconds, regardless of whether work, leisure, or goal is filled. A missing level does not block this close. There is no limit on the number of answers.
- Updated 2026-09-29: missing profile fields never extend the introduction beyond the speech threshold. Finish after processing the whole recording that reaches or crosses 120 seconds.
- Accept the recording that crosses a close condition in full.
- Silence, failed recognition, and technical failures before recognition do not spend the recording budget. Recognized non-English speech spends time but is not evidence of English proficiency. Onboarding uses STT language autodetection; ordinary English practice retains its English hint.
- Text gets the voice-message hint and is not analyzed. Voice before `Let’s chat 👋` gets the start-button hint and is not consumed.
- `/start` during an incomplete attempt resets it. Voice without `/start` continues the saved attempt. `/start` after completion uses the ordinary greeting.

## Implementation

Ktor owns Telegram rendering and serializes chat actions through reply delivery, with at most three admitted voice messages. AI-service owns the onboarding state machine. Existing STT, correction generation, LLM, TTS, polling jobs, usage metrics and streaks are reused; no new analytics or dashboards.

`onboarding:{sessionId}` stores one current attempt without the dialogue TTL: run id, state (`waiting`, `active`, `pending`, `completed`, `exempt`), recording duration, questions, transcripts, every correction from the turn (the live card still shows only the first by rank), compact word timings, the cached review, partial analyses, profile and final CEFR/text. The cached review keeps each skill's band, position, notes, and confidence. The public payload keeps the mapped score, the sentence, examples, and fluency measurements. Redis has an in-memory equivalent for tests. Restart/explicit reset replaces the attempt rather than archiving it. Raw audio is only transient in clip jobs, not archived. The daily minutes live in `practice-goal:{sessionId}`, also without a TTL, and are not part of the attempt.

Checkpoints prevent technical retries from counting or transcribing a recognized recording again. The current run id rejects stale buttons/jobs. Recent command and callback receipts are persisted (256 each per chat/attempt); the Telegram process also suppresses repeated incoming actions. Per-session AI locks follow the existing single-worker deployment model; horizontal multi-worker deployment requires distributed serialization first.

At the final boundary the attempt becomes `pending`. Failure to build the result produces `Retry`, which reuses the saved answers. Successful results are cached. Intermediate failures after recognition can also be retried with saved data; failure before recognition asks for another voice message. Telegram send/ack and Redis writes are not a distributed transaction; exactly-once external delivery is not guaranteed during a network loss.

On close, the introduction's recognized turns are copied into ordinary dialogue history, with the closing goodbye as the last reply. Later replies also receive a hidden note with work, free time, goal, and CEFR. The level may change how simple the English is, and it must not be spoken. The text slides may show the letter. Ordinary conversation does not update the profile. `/onboarding` replaces the attempt immediately, so the saved profile is gone until the new attempt completes. The daily-minutes choice stays. `/start` after completion does not reset it.

### Level explanation (2026-09-29)

The review receives the overall CEFR and its low/mid/high position alongside transcripts, corrections, and fluency signals. `levelText` explains demonstrated English in two or three short sentences with one or two brief exact transcript excerpts when supported. It must not recap conversation topics or praise ideas/personality. Limitations require observed evidence; unused constructions are not weaknesses. Thin or mixed evidence is acknowledged rather than forced to fit the band. A null CEFR gets an explanation of insufficient evidence, without assigning a level. Band selection and score mapping are unchanged. Existing saved reviews are not regenerated.
