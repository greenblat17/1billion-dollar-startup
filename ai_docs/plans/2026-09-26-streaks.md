# Streaks

Status: implemented (2026-09-26). Telegram bot and `/admin/metrics/streaks` only. The CMP app still uses the local mock.

## Decisions

- A Moscow day counts when `record_turn` runs (a finished clip, including the clarify line). That is the same signal as `metrics:dau:{day}`.
- No freeze. A missed day resets the current streak. `best` stays. The bot does not say the streak broke: a return is `Day 1`.
- The day boundary is `Europe/Moscow` for everyone.
- Past streaks are rebuilt once from `metrics:dau:*` (those keys have no TTL). The marker is `streak:backfill:v1`. The same run stores `streak:released_day` so retention can split before and after the release.
- After the voice reply, the first finished turn of the day gets a separate English message. Milestones 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, and 100 win over a new personal record. A record is only announced when the previous best was at least 2.
- `/streak` is in the bot command menu. It shows the current streak and seven squares, oldest first, today last.
- Reminders: streak of 2 or more uses `STREAK_REMINDER_TEMPLATES`. Everyone else keeps the original pool. `smile_today` moved into the streak pool; its id did not change.

## Redis

- `streak:user:{session}` hash: `current`, `best`, `last_day`, `first_day`. No TTL.
- `streak:users` set of sessions that have a streak row.
- Shown streak is `current` when `last_day` is today or yesterday, otherwise 0. Nothing clears it at midnight.
- Reminder day hashes gain `sent_streak_{bucket}` and `returned_streak_{bucket}`. Buckets: `0`, `1`, `2_6`, `7_13`, `14_plus`. The bucket is the shown streak at send time, stored on `reminder:last:{session}` as `streak_bucket`.

## HTTP

- Clip job `result.streak`: `{current, best, firstToday, firstEver, newRecord}` when the turn updated the streak. Absent on an older ai-service.
- `GET /internal/streak/{sessionId}` → `{current, best, last7: [bool x7]}`. `last7` is membership in `metrics:dau:{day}`.
- `POST /internal/reminders/claim` targets include `streak` (shown value, default 0).
- `GET /internal/metrics` adds `streaks`: `buckets`, `reminderBuckets` (7 days), `retention`.

## Retention

Weekly cohorts, Monday–Sunday Moscow, by `activated_day` (first voice). Last 8 weeks, newest first. D1, D7, and D30 are the share active on exactly `activated_day + N`. A cell is null until every member of that cohort has reached day N. `before` / `after` use `streak:released_day`. Bucket 0 on the tab includes Telegram users with no live streak, not only people who once had one.
