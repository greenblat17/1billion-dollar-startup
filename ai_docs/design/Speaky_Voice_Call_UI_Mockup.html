UI / UX proposal

Only what matters.

01Tutor avatar. The approved character is the center of the screen.
02Tutor name. Displayed under the avatar. “Anna” is a placeholder.

Agreed product rules
Tap Join Lesson in Telegram → Mini App opens and call begins automatically (subject to microphone permission). Ending the call returns to Telegram. No captions, waveforms, AI statuses, or post-call screens.
